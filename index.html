About

I am a Professor of Applied Economics at the University of Trento. My research explores the economics and geography of innovation, with particular attention to technological change, firms' capabilities, and the interaction between green and digital transitions.

My work connects firm-level innovation processes with regional development, knowledge networks and public policy.

Research interests

Green & digital transitions

The Twin Transition, environmental innovation, artificial intelligence and their complementarities.

Economic geography

Regional innovation systems, technological diversification, knowledge spillovers and spatial connections.

Firms & innovation

Firm capabilities, collaborations, investment and innovation policy.
